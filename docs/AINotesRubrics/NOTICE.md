The files were modified for Physlib: the verdicts (`approve`, `request_changes`, `block`) are
replaced by an advisory score from 0 to 5, findings are phrased as constructive suggestions,
the Tau Ceti roadmap is replaced by the Physlib contribution rules in `AGENTS.md` and
`docs/ReviewChecklist.md`, and a `physical-relevance` rubric is added. The git history of this
directory records the unmodified upstream files and every change made to them.

`references/naming-conventions.md` is the Mathlib naming guide, vendored by Tau Ceti from
<https://github.com/leanprover-community/leanprover-community.github.io>, with Tau Ceti's local
addendum.
