# Scope of Physlib and PhyslibAlpha

Scope is about whether a result fits into the project. This is vital,
and it is of high priority that all the results below are followed.

<a id="area-recognition"></a>

## 1. Area recognition

Every area added to Physlib or PhyslibAlpha should be established physics. That is, material taught in university physics courses, or an active research area with a body of peer-reviewed literature. This includes research-level and speculative areas, such as string theory, provided they are studied by a wider research community. It excludes personal or unpublished theories and fringe theories.

<a id="name-recognition"></a>

## 2. Declaration name recognition

Names of definitions, structures and well-known results should be ones physicists would recognize, such as `CanonicalEnsemble`, `partitionFunction` or `noether_theorem`. Don't invent names for objects or results that already have a standard name. Names that come from people, such as "Gibbs state", are fine when they are the standard name.

Formalizing physics sometimes needs a definition with no standard name in the literature. In that case, choose a descriptive name from which a physicist can guess the meaning, and explain the definition and why it is needed in its docstring.
