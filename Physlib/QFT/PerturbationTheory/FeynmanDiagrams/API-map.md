# Feynman diagrams

## Abstract (Alpha)

A Feynman diagram is a partition of a multiset of field operators into disjoint pairs: the
multiset counterpart of a Wick contraction, which pairs up positions in a list of field
operators. Passing from lists to multisets makes a diagram independent of the order in which the
operators are written down, and the resulting many-to-one map from contractions to diagrams is
what a symmetry factor accounts for. The area rests on the existing perturbation-theory
material: the field specification fixes the fields of a theory, their bosonic or fermionic
statistics and the labels from which field operators and their creation and annihilation parts
are built; the free operator algebra and the Wick algebra are where the value of a diagram is
computed; and Wick contractions supply the pairings of a list with their Koszul signs, time
contractions and Wick's theorem. A position field operator carries a point of spacetime, a
Lorentz vector acted on by the Lorentz group.
