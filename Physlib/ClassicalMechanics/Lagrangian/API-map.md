# Lagrangian mechanics

## Abstract

Lagrangian systems on a smooth manifold and the critical points of their action.

## Content

A Lagrangian system is a smooth function on time and the tangent bundle. [1] The action of a
curve integrates it along the velocity; a critical point is a smooth curve whose action is
stationary under every variation fixing the endpoints. [2]

A smooth curve is a critical point if and only if the Euler–Lagrange equations hold in every
chart. [planned] If an autonomous system admits a one-parameter group of diffeomorphisms, its
Lagrange equations have a first integral (Noether). [planned] The energy of an autonomous system
is conserved. [planned] A natural system has Lagrangian kinetic minus potential energy, from a
Riemannian metric. [planned]

Lagrangians `L(q, q̇, t)` in generalised coordinates are Lagrangian systems. [planned] Momentum
and angular momentum conservation follow from Noether's theorem. [planned]
